The Last Checkout

Up to four guests. One stretched bellhop. Haul valuables back to your rooms and make quota before checkout.

    How to play
    WASD
    Move · Shift sprint · Space jump
    Click
    Grab an item so it floats ahead of you · click again to set it down
    Right-click

    E
    Front desk shop · ring the checkout bell to end the shift early
    Noise
    He hunts by sound. Walking is quiet, sprinting carries, and anything thrown or dropped hard is loud. Every noise he hears makes him angrier and faster. Hard hits also damage the item.
    Computer
    Hold E at the desk in your room to boot the security computer, then press E to watch the hotel's old cameras.
    M
    Raise the hotel map: your position, your team, and where he was last heard
    F · 1 · 2
    Flashlight · throw a flare (stuns him) · drink a soda
    Quota
    Only items resting inside your team's rooms (101–104) count. Make quota before the clock runs out.
